1. If you (heat) ice, it (melt).

2. If I (have) enough money, I (buy) a new car.

3. If she (study) harder last semester, she (pass) the exam.

4. If it (rain) tomorrow, we (not go) to the park.

5. If I (know) his number, I (call) him.
